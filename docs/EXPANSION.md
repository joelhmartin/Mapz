# Expansion queue

Each item is a large chunk of work for one scheduled run (every two days). Take the first unchecked item, build all of it, validate it in the browser, push to `main`, and tick it off here with the date and a one-line summary of what shipped. If an item proves too large, ship a coherent first half, then split the remainder into a new item directly below it.

- [x] **The Americas.** _Done 2026-10-07:_ about 300 nations, cultures and states across the Americas from the Archaic to the 1880s — historical-basemaps snapshots (named Native nations at 1492–1715, Andean and Mesoamerican states, culture regions), 43 curated entries (Mississippian centres, Hohokam, Wendat, Powhatan, contested post-1783 nations from the Cherokee to the Nez Perce, Tlaxcala, Kʼicheʼ, Muisca, Mapuche, Palmares, the Taíno and Kalinago), and a `contested` flag that draws nations over colonial claims.
- [x] **Migrations and voyages layer.** _Done 2026-10-09:_ 25 routes that draw themselves along the timeline with a moving arrowhead and fade after they end — the Bantu expansion, Austronesian and Lapita voyages, the settlement of East Polynesia, Hawaiʻi, Rapa Nui, Aotearoa and Rēkohu, the voyage to Madagascar, Norse voyages, Alexander, Hannibal, four Mongol campaigns, the Mfecane (Ndebele, Gaza, Ngoni, Kololo), the Great Trek, the Trail of Tears and the Long Walk; hover card, route panel with clickable itinerary, and a movements toggle.
- [x] **More movements.** _Done 2026-10-11:_ 30 more routes (55 in all) and recurring routes drawn whole for their active span — Indo-European migrations, the early Muslim and Umayyad conquests, the First and Third Crusades, Viking raids and the Varangian route, Columbus, da Gama, Magellan–Elcano, Zheng He, Marco Polo, Ibn Battuta, Napoleon in Egypt and Russia, the Silk Road, trans-Saharan, Incense and Indian Ocean trade routes, five main routes of the Atlantic slave trade, and the Partition of India.
- [x] **Wars layer.** _Done 2026-10-13:_ 1,787 wars and campaigns from Wikidata (in the battles workflow); 517 top-level wars draw theatres — rounded hulls around regional clusters of their battles, per decade for long wars — while active; hover and click open a war panel with dates, parent war, combatants and its battles in order, with previous/next stepping that moves the timeline and flies the map; battle panels link to their wars; a wars toggle. Implausible battle links (outside the war's own dates) are dropped.
- [x] **Asia gap-filling.** _Done 2026-10-15:_ `data/curated/asia.json` — about 110 historical-basemaps names for Asia, renamed to match Cliopatria where they are the same state (Indus Valley, BMAC, Andronovo and other cultures, the Mahajanapadas, Saka, Sarmatians, Türgesh, Pechenegs, Dvaravati, Mon city-states, Arakan, the Gajapatis, Gujarat, Berar, Golconda, the Aravidu remnant of Vijayanagara and more), plus 40 curated heartlands: the Tarim kingdoms (Khotan, Kucha, Karasahr, Shule, Loulan), Kangju, the Afrighids, the Alchon Huns, the Keraites, Naimans, Merkits, Tatars and Khamag Mongol before 1206, Abu'l-Khayr's Uzbeks, Buyeo, Kofun-era Yamato, Langkasuka, Tambralinga, Butuan, Ternate, the Ahom, Kamata, Manipur and Sikkim, six Rajput kingdoms, the Gujarat, Malwa, Khandesh and Madurai sultanates, the Reddys, Keladi and Gingee Nayaks, and Kandy (drawn over the colonial claim). Curated polities are now clipped era by era against Cliopatria and trimmed to the coastline. Cliopatria already maps Funan, Srivijaya, the Khmer, Champa, Đại Việt, Majapahit, Ayutthaya, Pagan, the Xiongnu, Göktürks, Uyghurs, the Golden Horde's successors, the Kazakh Khanate and the Maratha Empire, so those were left to it.
- [x] **Rulers and dynasties.** _Done 2026-10-17:_ c. 6,900 reigns for c. 580 polities from Wikidata, fetched in a new `update-rulers.yml` workflow (head-of-state statements, holders of each polity's head-of-state office, offices whose jurisdiction is the polity, and generic offices qualified with it; consorts, heirs, electors and prelates filtered out; a person's overlapping terms merged). The info panel shows who ruled in the selected year, a dynasty band and a scrollable reign timeline that follows the year; clicking a reign moves the map to it. Ends missing in Wikidata are inferred from the next accession and marked. Coverage follows Wikidata: many African, American and early states have no ruler data yet.
- [x] **Hand-drawn look.** _Done 2026-10-19:_ every border, coast, lake and river wobbles by one shared noise field applied at build time (long edges densified first, so neighbours still meet); a watercolour wash over the land with pigment pooling inside each state's edges; inkier, softer borders; engraved hatching on sea and lakes and water-lining along coasts from zoom 3; a printed cartouche for the title and a compass rose (map rotation is now off, so north stays up). The wash sits on the static basemap rather than the polities source, keeping the year step within c. 10–15% of before in a software-GL benchmark.
- [x] **Search and story mode.** _Done 2026-10-21:_ a search box (press "/") over every state, ruler, battle, war and movement, which moves the timeline into the result's span, flies to it and opens its panel; and five guided tours with 39 narrated steps — The rise of the Zulu, Polynesian voyaging, Mali and Songhai, The Mongol Empire, and Rome: republic, empire and Byzantium — each step setting the year, flying the map and outlining the state in focus, with Wikipedia links and deep links (`?tour=zulu&step=3`).
