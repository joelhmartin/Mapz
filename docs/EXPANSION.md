# Expansion queue

Each item is a large chunk of work for one scheduled run (every two days). Take the first unchecked item, build all of it, validate it in the browser, push to `main`, and tick it off here with the date and a one-line summary of what shipped. If an item proves too large, ship a coherent first half, then split the remainder into a new item directly below it.

- [ ] **The Americas.** Native nations and empires from the Archaic to the 19th century: Mississippian chiefdoms (Cahokia), Ancestral Puebloans, the Haudenosaunee Confederacy, the Powhatan, Cherokee, Muscogee and Lakota, Comanchería, Taíno cacicazgos, Aztec tributary provinces, Inca expansion phases (Pachacuti → Huayna Capac), the Mapuche, plus colonial frontiers. Use historical-basemaps for the Americas the way Africa uses it, and curated entries for the rest.
- [ ] **Migrations and voyages layer.** A new animated layer of arrows that draw themselves along the timeline: the Bantu expansion, Polynesian voyaging, the Mfecane (Ndebele, Shangane, Ngoni, Kololo), Norse voyages, the Mongol campaigns, the Great Trek. Data in `data/curated/migrations.json`; hover and click like battles.
- [ ] **Wars layer.** Fetch wars from Wikidata (in GitHub Actions, like battles) with date ranges and participants. Draw each war's theatre as a soft hull around its battles while it is active; a war panel lists its battles in order and lets you step through them on the timeline.
- [ ] **Asia gap-filling.** Southeast Asia (Funan, Srivijaya, Khmer Empire, Champa, Đại Việt, Majapahit, Ayutthaya, Pagan), the steppe (Xiongnu, Göktürks, Uyghurs, the Golden Horde's successors, the Kazakh Khanate), and South Asian regional powers (Rajput states, the Maratha Confederacy, the Vijayanagara successors), using the Africa method.
- [ ] **Rulers and dynasties.** Fetch heads of state with dates from Wikidata (in Actions) for every polity that has a Wikidata id; the info panel shows who ruled in the selected year and a scrollable reign timeline.
- [ ] **Hand-drawn look.** Wobbly, inked borders (geometry jittered at build time), watercolour-textured fills, a compass rose, sea texture and a decorative cartouche, without breaking the performance rules.
- [ ] **Search and story mode.** A search box for polities, battles and rulers, and guided tours ("The rise of the Zulu", "Polynesian voyaging", "Mali and Songhai") that move the map and timeline step by step with narration.
- [ ] **Europe, Middle East and Caucasus gap-filling.** Pre-state and tribal peoples (Celts, Germanic peoples, Slavs, Baltic tribes), Caucasus principalities and Arabian tribal confederations where Cliopatria is thin.
- [ ] **Performance stage 2.** Split polity tiles into era slices so a year change only touches the slice for that era; measure year-step → idle latency before and after.
